## Services

### 1. Leadership Transition & Executive Integration Coaching

Your signature specialty for newly promoted executives, leaders entering new organizations, or taking on expanded scope.

This coaching supports the leader before, during, and beyond the crucial early months. It is tailored to the situation rather than presented as a rigid 90-day program, helping leaders reduce uncertainty, avoid early missteps, build trust, and establish lasting effectiveness.

### 2. Executive Onboarding Support

For executives entering critical roles, new markets, or expanded responsibility.

## For Organizations

**Heading:** Strengthen Leadership at Critical Moments

Leadership transitions carry significant expectations for both the executive and the organization. When alignment is slow, priorities remain unclear, or stakeholder relationships are strained, the consequences can extend well beyond the individual leader.

Trostli Leadership works with organizations to support executives in critical roles, strengthen senior team effectiveness, and create the conditions for sustainable performance.

Through tailored coaching and leadership development, we help organizations:

- Accelerate executive integration and early effectiveness.
- Strengthen trust and alignment among key stakeholders.
- Reduce avoidable friction during leadership transitions.
- Support leadership continuity and the development of critical talent.
- Build stronger collaboration and decision-making across teams.

The objective is practical: helping leaders succeed while strengthening the teams and organizations that depend on them.
